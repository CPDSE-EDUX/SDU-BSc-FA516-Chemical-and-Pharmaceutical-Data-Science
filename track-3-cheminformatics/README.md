# Track 3: Cheminformatics

How a molecule becomes numbers in the FA516 course: SMILES read and written by hand, what
stereochemistry looks like in a string, the descriptors that summarise a structure, and the
fingerprints that let you ask how similar two compounds are.

This is the track the machine learning in tracks 4 to 6 is fed from.

Assumes tracks 1 and 2.

## Topics

- **SMILES & stereochemistry**: reading and writing SMILES, stereochemistry in SMILES
- **Molecular descriptors**: what a descriptor is, physicochemical descriptors and drug-likeness, descriptor tables, 3D descriptors and shape
- **Fingerprints**: what a fingerprint is, Morgan/ECFP, Tanimoto similarity, SMARTS substructure search, grouping compounds by similarity
